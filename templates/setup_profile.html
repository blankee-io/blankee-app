Secure Your Account

Add an extra layer of security with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). You'll use an authenticator app to generate a code each time you log in.

  1. Install an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or Authy) on your phone.
  2. Open the app and choose "Add Account" or "Scan QR Code".
  3. Scan the QR code below with your app.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code from your app to verify.
