A hidden basket id, empty for an add, is the whole difference. And one copy across pages, for the same reason: Manage Baskets and the month view both open this, and two copies would be the same trap one level up. Behaviour lives in static/js/loaf_basket_modal.js, which a page loads and then calls loafBasketModal.openAdd() or .openEdit(id) from whatever opened it. The weekday lists ride on the element below so that file needs no Jinja. A page including this must also render the two things the form depends on: alert_modals.html, for showToast, and the stylesheet's .income-form-container family, which it borrows from the recurring forms. It must pass `today`, for the pay date's lower bound. TWO FIELDS ARE GONE AND THE COLUMNS ARE NOT. `as of` - the hours you have are the hours you have now, so the server stamps starting_date itself whenever that figure changes. `maximum balance` - max_balance_hours still exists and the projection still honours it, so a basket that has one keeps behaving as it did. Nothing sets one any more, which means the ceiling effect (accrual stopping at a cap, and time off making room for it) is out of reach from the UI. #}
