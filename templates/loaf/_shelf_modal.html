{# Times rather than a total per day, because "off Tuesday from 1pm" cannot be costed without them. Off is a checkbox rather than "clear both boxes": two empty fields read as unfinished, and the engine treats them as a day not worked either way - so the box says out loud what the blanks only implied. The columns are still per day, and the projection still reads them per day, so a week with genuinely different breaks stays expressible - this form just writes the same figure to all seven. #} {# Three states a working day can be in beyond its hours, and they are genuinely different: Off not counted at all. Not worked, not in the denominator, nothing to book. Free worked and counted like any other day, and never billed. Take the week off and this day is not charged - but you were still away for it, so it still reduces the hours the next accrual is pro-rated by. Not in never attended at all: a compressed week the employer accrues as a standard one. Costs nothing AND loses nothing, because there were no hours there to lose. Free and Not in are the pair that get confused, and they disagree on exactly one question - were you at work? Getting it wrong is quiet: the balance comes out right either way and only the accrual drifts. Not in stays behind the counted-week flag - see loafBasketModal.countedWeek - because the schedule it is for is rare. Free does not: an employer that does not bill one weekday is an ordinary arrangement. #}
