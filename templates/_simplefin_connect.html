{# The SimpleFIN connect flow, used by the setup wizard (step 3) and the bank page. Expects `bank` = get_bank_provider().connect_widget_config(user_id) and `sf_mode` = 'setup' | 'page' | 'replace'. static/js/simplefin_connect.js drives it; the page that includes this calls SimpleFINConnect.init({ onLinked: fn }). Three numbered cards because that is what the user actually has to do, in that order, on a site that is not ours: SimpleFIN has no widget and no redirect back - the token arrives in their clipboard and gets pasted here. #}

You are already connected to SimpleFIN. Your accounts are below - say what each one is and press Save accounts. Only paste a token if you want to replace the connection.

1

Create your SimpleFIN account

SimpleFIN Bridge is the service that talks to your bank. It is yours, not Blankee's: you sign up with your email, subscribe (about $1.50 a month or $15 a year), then add each bank under Financial Institutions → New Connection.

Open SimpleFIN Bridge
2

Make a Setup Token

On the Bridge, go to Apps → New Connection, name it Blankee, and press Create Setup Token. Copy the token it shows you. It works once, so make a new one if you ever need to connect again.

3

Paste it here

Blankee turns the token into its own connection to SimpleFIN and shows your accounts. The token itself is not kept.

Your accounts

SimpleFIN does not say what kind of account each one is, so tell Blankee. One checking and one savings account can be imported, and any number of credit cards. Choose Don't import for the rest.
